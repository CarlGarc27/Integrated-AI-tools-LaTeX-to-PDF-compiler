# Finiteness of the class group of a number field

Subproject of the PhD project. The general rules in the root `CLAUDE.md` apply.

## Structure

- `demostraciones/` — LaTeX proofs and their PDFs.
- `revisiones/` — review reports `ronda-N.md` and the log `log.md` from the
  `revisor-matematico` subagent.

## Goal

Prove that the ideal class group of a number field is finite, via the
Minkowski bound.
